# A website for personal projects

I use it for learning and testing.
I use it for my own personal projects that I have developed to different degrees at different times.
There is no clear structure or order to the projects. The only thing they have in common is that I have been involved in them at some point in my life.
For some reason I spent some amount of time and energy working on them. 
They were, at least in part, my creation, which means there must be some ṕart of myself in them.
They must have something to say about me and who I am or was.
I guess their apparent randomness is a sort of reflection of myself at different times of my life: my ideas, feelings, beliefs, interests, etc.
I wonder what would happen if I continue to fill it with my projects through my whole life. What would all that amount to?
What conclusion would someone reach if they were to do an analysis of the whole? What kind of person would they think I was? 
Would there finally be an overarching cohesion? Would it just be a random collection of thoughts from a dispersed attention?
This got deeper than intended.
I value that side of myself that enojoys tinkering and learning. 
Maybe that's what this is.
A digital monument to my playful self.
